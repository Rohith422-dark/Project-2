# Smart Community Health Monitoring & Early Warning System

A smart, AI-enabled health surveillance platform designed to monitor water quality, detect early signs of water-borne disease outbreaks, and enable timely interventions through coordinated action between health officials, ASHA workers, and community members.

---

## Project Overview

Water-borne diseases such as diarrhea, cholera, typhoid, and hepatitis A pose significant public health challenges, especially in rural and semi-urban regions. Delayed detection of contaminated water sources and lack of real-time health surveillance often result in large-scale outbreaks.

This project provides a **centralized digital platform** that integrates:
- Real-time water quality monitoring
- Community health reporting
- AI-powered outbreak prediction
- Emergency alert management
- Health education and intervention tracking

---

## Key Objectives

- Monitor water quality parameters and detect contamination early
- Enable real-time reporting of health issues from communities
- Predict potential disease outbreaks using analytics
- Support rapid response through alerts and interventions
- Improve public awareness through health education resources

---

## System Modules

### 1. Health Surveillance Dashboard
- Real-time health reports and patient tracking
- Critical alerts and affected district monitoring
- Prevention score and analytics insights
- AI-powered outbreak risk detection

### 2. Water Quality Monitoring
- Tracks pH level, turbidity, temperature, and bacterial content
- Classifies water sources as Safe, Contaminated, or Under Testing
- Displays safety standards and precautionary guidelines

### 3. Active Alerts & Emergency Response
- Real-time alerts for water contamination and disease outbreaks
- Severity-based prioritization
- Response status tracking and resolution metrics
- Exportable alert reports

### 4. Interventions Management
- Monitoring of ongoing and planned health programs
- Progress and effectiveness tracking
- Beneficiary management
- AI-based recommendations for timing, budget, and resource optimization

### 5. Health Records Management
- Patient portfolios with case status
- Completed, pending, and high-priority case tracking
- Location-based health data organization

### 6. Health Education Center
- Educational video resources on water-borne diseases
- Prevention techniques and symptom awareness
- Categorized content for easy access

### 7. Community Health Portal
- Community-level health dashboards
- Reporting of health and water-related issues
- Emergency contacts and advisories
- Access to local ASHA workers and support services

---

## AI & Analytics

- Uses historical health data and water quality trends
- Identifies early outbreak risks
- Supports data-driven decision-making
- Improves intervention effectiveness and response time
